# Climate pathways and historical FABLE-C coefficients

## Two different scientific products

**Pathway-resolved scientific IWR release:** the original 29,338-row accepted net master retains `RCP26` (RCP2.6) and `RCP60` (RCP6.0) for scientific sensitivity and gross-withdrawal calculations. Each pathway is a separate scientific output. These are **not** claims that both RCPs were deployed in the operational FABLE Calculator.

**Historical FABLE-C export:** the accepted September repaired historical net IWR dataset is scenario-neutral and is merged to the corrected historical SPAM key universe. It is **not** regenerated by blindly averaging every RCP26/RCP60 pair in the pathway-resolved master. Different historical scientific routes and completion decisions have been used.

| 2020 | Equal-weight mean of `RCP26` and `RCP60` |

For 2010/2015/2020, the original direct-crop routine required **both** scenarios and failed if one was missing; it did not silently select the available scenario. This applies to that specified direct LPJmL processing component, not an automatic universal rule for all 56 product routes. The original later work combined 12 direct crops, nine frozen proxies, external evidence and physically reconstructed estimates, then repaired missing historical IWR states against corrected SPAM.

## Export rule adopted in this repository

`08-export-fablec-historical.py` reads the **already accepted scenario-neutral repaired historical IWR file** and produces a 7-column FABLE3 IWR export, together with a separate matched SPAM panel and gross coefficient diagnostic. It does not reselect pathways or invent means for unsupported crop routes. The historical direct LPJmL methodology is documented here for scientific provenance, not silently reapplied a second time.

Before claiming end-to-end ISIMIP → historical FABLE-C reconstruction, separately regenerate the 56-product repaired historical net IWR dataset and numerically compare with its original accepted reference. No source-data or historical-average equality claim is made by the new exporter alone.
